UKUTA is the collective voice of UK universities engaged with Türkiye — committed to building high-level, strategic connections between UK higher education and Türkiye's dynamic education landscape. We bring together universities, education professionals, schools, and student advisors to create meaningful, lasting pathways between two countries with a shared ambition for academic excellence.
